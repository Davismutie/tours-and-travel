GAME PARKS

Where the wildlife actually is.

Four parks we build most itineraries around — each chosen for a different kind of sighting.

TANZANIA

Serengeti National Park

Endless plains and the highest predator density in East Africa — lion, leopard and cheetah are daily sightings, not lucky ones. Best timed around the wildebeest migration, June through October.

TANZANIA

Ngorongoro Crater

An intact volcanic caldera holding one of Africa's densest concentrations of wildlife year-round, including a resident black rhino population. A full day on the crater floor is standard.

UGANDA

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Dense montane rainforest home to roughly half the world's remaining mountain gorillas. Treks run in small permitted groups, guided by trackers who know each family by name.

KENYA

Maasai Mara

The Mara River crossings are the single most dramatic sighting on the migration route — wildebeest and zebra against crocodile-filled water, framed by open savanna in every direction.
